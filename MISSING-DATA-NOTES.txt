FABMATCH SITE: WHAT IS MISSING FROM THE REDESIGN COLLECTIVE SAMPLES
==================================================================
Source: DATASET_FABRIC_SAMPLES.xlsx, sheet "Higher-End Textiles"
Added: 91 sample pieces as lots RC-0001 to RC-0091, one per spreadsheet row, in sheet order.
Data file: server/data/seed/redesign-collective-samples.json   Photos: public/samples/

WHAT EACH SAMPLE HAS (from the spreadsheet)
- Brand (shown as "Brand: X, from the sample label, not verified")
- Piece size in inches, quantity in pieces, inventory code, NAICS code, photo
- A name and title the site requires, made from the brand: "Pindler sample 48"

MISSING FOR ALL 91 SAMPLES (left blank; the site shows "Not recorded")
- Product or pattern name (the sheet has only the brand, so titles are "Brand sample N")
- Application: upholstery, drapery, wallcovering, acoustic (category chips and Art-ready skip these lots)
- Fiber content (fiber filter and search)
- Color family and tone from the 12-family palette (color and tone filters skip these lots)
- Pattern (herringbone, plaid, and so on)
- Direction: one-way or may be turned. Until it is recorded, product estimates do NOT turn the pieces (cautious)
- Fire rating, abrasion (double rubs), acoustic NRC
- Documentation on file (technical data, fiber content, fire test report)
- Verified manufacturer status (brands come from labels, so they are not counted as verified manufacturers)
- Region (lots say "Seattle region"; the sheet does not say where the samples are)
- Last confirmed date (cards say "Not confirmed yet"; they sort last under Newest)
- Status: set to "In inventory" as a placeholder. Confirm whether these are still on hand.

HOW TO FILL IN A MISSING ATTRIBUTE
Edit server/data/seed/redesign-collective-samples.json only for brand, size, quantity, or photo. For the other
attributes, edit the lot in server/.data/lots.json (fields: cat, fiber, fam, shade, tex, pattern, directional,
fire, rubs, nrc, docs, region, lastConfirmed) and restart the server. Field formats match the FM- lots in that file.
